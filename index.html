Estado actual

Understand the project before you visit

Entienda el proyecto antes de visitarla

Both engines are out of service and partially disassembled. Injectors have been removed and are not validated for reinstatement. No engine start, load test or sea trial was performed during the July inspection. Engine hours are unknown.

The propellers physically exist, but are removed and in poor condition; replacement is expected for regular operation. Navigation equipment is not available for operation, and the autopilot is reported nonfunctional. Engine electronics and connected systems require further diagnosis. The final repair scope, cost and duration have not been established.

Cosmetic wear remains, including marks on flybridge seating and cloudy/discolored clear enclosure panels. The condition report lists no refrigerator, no TVs and no installed sound system included; the freezer is separate. Generator operation has not been verified in the recovered inspection records.

Read the condition disclosure. Bring your own surveyor and technical team.

Ambos motores están fuera de servicio y parcialmente desarmados. Los inyectores fueron retirados y no están validados para su reinstalación. Durante la inspección de julio no se realizó arranque de motor, prueba de carga ni prueba de mar. Las horas de motor se desconocen.
